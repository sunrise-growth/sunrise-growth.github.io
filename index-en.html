Travel accessories旅行周邊

On every journey, what matters most is being there with you.

Sunrise Growth’s own brand of travel accessories.

What we do

A growing range for the whole trip: getting ready before you leave, what you need on the move, and settling in when you arrive.

The idea behind the brand is simple: make travel gear that fits how people actually travel, so they can save their energy for the journey itself.

Approach

Every product is planned around four principles.

Easy to organize
Packs neatly, and is easy to find and reach when you need it.
Easy to carry
Size and weight are weighed carefully to lighten the load.
Easy to use
Clear and intuitive to operate.
Easy to pair
Made to work with what you already bring along.
Progress

Within three months of its founding, the brand had put more than ten products into mass production.

The first is the DAWNVIAN 200W GaN Smart Universal Travel Charger. It combines a travel adapter and a charger in one, so your laptop, phone and tablet can charge together abroad, with one less thing in your bag.

Coming next

Next is a travel vacuum storage bag, with the pump built into the bag itself.

Pack your clothes, draw out the air and they press down flat, with no separate pump to carry. It comes in two designs.

Digital products數位產品

App development

Apps for health, lifestyle and fitness.

What we do

We start from everyday life. Our first, Wenjiao (問筊), is a lifestyle app that lets you experience Taiwanese temple culture with your own hands.

Approach

We respect people’s time and privacy. Here is how Wenjiao does it:

No sign-up
Open it and start. No account needed.
Data stays on the phone
Records and personal details are stored only on your own iPhone.
No ads, no tracking
No advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.
Made for iPhone
A Traditional Chinese interface, built for iPhone.
Coming next

Our first app, Wenjiao (問筊), is expected on the App Store in early January 2027.

A simulation and hands-on experience of Taiwanese temple culture: 3D moon blocks, an altar table you set with your own offerings, four sets of poem lots with plain-language readings, villain hitting (a folk custom of beating a paper figure to let off steam), and the lunar calendar, solar terms and a daily quote.

Principles經營理念

Three businesses, one way of working.

Practical
We start from real needs: understand when and how people will use something, then decide what to make and what to leave out.
Quality
We take care of the details, and would rather take a little longer so that what reaches customers holds up in use.
Trust
We only promise what we can deliver, and we deliver what we promise. Long-term trust is built one exchange at a time.
Privacy
We respect everyone’s privacy: we take only the information we truly need, and leave the rest.
