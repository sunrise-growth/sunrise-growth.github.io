問筊Wenjiao

A simulation and hands-on experience of Taiwanese temple culture.

iPhone App
Expected on the App Store in early January 2027

Features功能

Moon blocks, worship, poem lots, villain hitting, and the lunar calendar and solar terms every day.

It follows the steps you would take at a temple: choose a deity, set out offerings, light incense, throw the moon blocks, draw a poem lot. You do every step with your own hands.

Moon blocks
Tap, swipe up, or hold your phone and shake it, and a pair of 3D moon blocks tumbles, lands and bounces, with sound and vibration on every impact. In standard asking, one throw gives you a result; in careful asking, you need three shengbei in a row.
Temple keeper
Not sure how it works? Ask the temple keeper: how to throw, how to phrase a question, what to do if a block lands standing up. Eight common questions, answered with a tap.
Worship
Each of the twelve deities has an altar table of their own. Drag any of more than 140 offerings, from fruit and cakes to flowers and joss paper, to wherever you like on the table, then place the incense in the burner and hold the screen with your palms together.
Practice
Strike the wooden fish and meditate to earn devotion points for offerings; devotion points can’t be bought. There are nine practice ranks, and each new rank adds decorations and styles to the altar table.
Poem lots
Tap the lot cylinder on the altar table to draw a lot from the deity in front of you. Four sets of poem lots (Sixty Jiazi, Guanyin 100, Guandi 100 and Yue Lao) are shown in vertical text, with a plain-language summary, reminders and notes on specific areas of life.
Villain hitting
A folk custom found in Hong Kong and Taiwan, usually around the solar term Jingzhe in early spring: you beat a paper figure to let off steam. Choose from five paper villains and thirty items, or use your hands; when you’ve had enough, send him into the brazier. A nickname written on his chest is never saved.
Calendar
A calendar page for every day: the date, lunar date, stem-branch year and zodiac animal, the 24 solar terms, festivals, deity birthdays, and the 1st and 15th of the lunar month. The daily quote changes each time you open the app, and you can turn on a daily quote reminder and solar term reminders.
History
Note down what you asked, and add what happened later. Filter your history, view statistics, or turn saving off altogether.
Widgets
Put the Today widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Tap the Moon blocks widget to throw once without opening the app.

Privacy資料與隱私

We do not collect any data.

  • Your personal details, moon block and poem lot records, and worship data are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to our servers or anyone else’s.
  • No analytics tools, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone. Motion sensing is used only to tell whether you are shaking the phone, and is never stored or sent. An image is saved to your photo library only when you choose Save Image.
  • You can clear your records and worship data at any time in the app’s settings, and deleting the app deletes all of its data.
  • Payments to support the team are handled by Apple; we cannot access your payment details.
