Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Wenjiao (問筊) is provided by Sunrise Growth Co., Ltd. (旭禾山有限公司, “we” or “us”). You do not need to create an account to use Wenjiao, and this version of the app does not send any of your data to our servers or anyone else’s. This policy explains what the app stores on your phone, which permissions it uses, and how you can manage and delete that data.

If there is any inconsistency, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Wenjiao iPhone app (the “App”) and to sunrise-growth.com, the website that introduces and supports it.

The policy shown in the App under 設定 › 資料與隱私 › 隱私權政策 (Settings › Data & Privacy › Privacy Policy) is the same text as the Chinese version on this website.

2. Who we are

The App is provided by Sunrise Growth Co., Ltd., a limited company in Taiwan that is responsible for the App and for this policy. If you have any questions about privacy, you can write to us at the address in the last section, “Contact us.”

3. What we collect

We do not collect any data. Everything listed below stays on your iPhone, and we cannot see it.

The App does not send data to our servers, and we do not use your data for advertising or analytics, or to build any kind of profile.

4. Data stored on your phone

  • Personal details: the first time you open the App, the temple keeper asks what you would like to be called, your gender, your birthday and your birth hour. Every question can be skipped. If you answer, they are used to address you on your name card, in greetings and in the sample wording for stating your request; your gender also decides how your practice rank title is written. You can change or clear them in 設定 › 個人資料 (Settings › Personal details).
  • Moon block and poem lot records: the time, deity and result of each throw or draw, plus any question and follow-up notes you write yourself.
  • Worship data: devotion points, the offerings you have placed, your incense and worship records, consecutive days of worship, your practice rank, and how many times you have struck the wooden fish and meditated.
  • Villain hitting: only the number of paper villains you have sent off this year, and which villain you picked last time. A nickname you write on a villain and the tags you choose last only for that round, are cleared once you send it off, and are never saved.
  • Settings: preferences such as appearance, text size, sound, which reminders are on, and which deities’ birthdays to be reminded of.
  • Widgets: the home screen widgets keep their own state on your phone and share the daily quote setting with the App.

The App does not record what you say silently when stating your request.

5. How your data is protected

This data is kept in the App’s own storage on your iPhone and protected by iOS security, so other apps cannot read it. The App does not send data off your phone, so it is never transmitted over the internet.

Anyone who can unlock your phone can also open the App and see these records. Please set a passcode on your iPhone and keep your phone safe.

6. How long data is kept and how to delete it

This data stays on your phone until you delete it.

  • 設定 › 資料與隱私 › 清除所有紀錄 (Settings › Data & Privacy › Clear all records): deletes your moon block and poem lot records.
  • 設定 › 個人資料 (Settings › Personal details): clears your personal details.
  • Deleting the App deletes all of its data, including your worship data (devotion points, practice, offerings collection and worship records).

If you use iCloud Backup on your iPhone, your system backup may include this data. That backup is handled by Apple according to your system settings.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, you can manage it yourself, directly on your phone:

  • Access: moon block and poem lot records are in 設定 › 請示紀錄 (Settings › History); worship data is in 參拜足跡 (Worship journal) on the worship tab; personal details are in 設定 › 個人資料 (Settings › Personal details).
  • Correction: you can change your personal details at any time, and edit saved questions and follow-up notes in each record.
  • Deletion: use the steps in the previous section to delete a single record, clear all your moon block and poem lot records, or delete the App to clear all of its data.

Since we do not have your data, we cannot look it up, change it or delete it for you. If you have any questions about this, please write to us.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders for the daily quote, solar terms, and worship days such as deity birthdays, Tianshe Day, the 1st and 15th of the lunar month, festivals and Zuoya are all scheduled locally by your iPhone and never pass through a server. The App asks whether you want them the first time you use it, and you can turn each one off in 設定 › 聲音與提醒 (Settings › Sound & reminders) at any time.
  • Motion sensing: when you shake your phone to throw moon blocks or draw a lot, the App reads the phone’s acceleration and rotation only to tell whether you are shaking it. This is not stored or sent anywhere. If you turn off 設定 › 擲筊與抽籤 › 搖一搖手機來擲筊、抽籤 (Settings › Moon blocks & poem lots › Shake to throw and draw), the App stops reading it.
  • Photos: the App saves an image to your photo library only when you choose 儲存影像 (Save Image) on the share screen. The App does not read your photos.

The App does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

9. In-app purchases and membership

Payments for "Support the developers" and the WenJiao membership are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details, and we do not know who made a purchase.

To find out whether you are a member, the app asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether this Apple Account has an active subscription. The answer is used only to decide whether to show member content and is never sent to us.

Subscriptions renew automatically. You can cancel at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone; refunds are requested from Apple under Apple’s rules.

If you choose to be reminded the day before a free trial ends, that notification is scheduled on your iPhone and does not pass through any server.

Payments are completed through Apple’s payment screen while you are online; how Apple handles your payment information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

10. Third parties

This version does not use any analytics tools, advertising or third-party SDKs. The Kai and Song typefaces used on screen are system fonts that iOS downloads from Apple; they do not come from us.

Contact us and Support website in the App open your iPhone’s Mail app or web browser. Once you leave the App, those apps and websites are governed by their own policies.

11. This website

sunrise-growth.com is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising or tracking code, and it has no forms for you to fill in.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the server handles technical connection data (such as your IP address) when it delivers a page. That data is handled by GitHub under its own policies, and we do not have access to it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and use them only to reply to you.

12. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

The App needs no account and does not require you to enter any information. Every question the temple keeper asks, such as your name or birthday, can be skipped.

13. Updates to this policy

If we ever add a feature that sends data off your phone, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent in the App.

Whenever we change this policy, we update the “Last updated” date at the top. This version takes effect on November 28, 2026.

14. Contact us

If you have any questions, please write to: service@sunrise-growth.com
